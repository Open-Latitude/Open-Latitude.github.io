Before you choose

The practical details.

Read the listing and preview for the
specific pack before purchasing.

Which age or level should I choose?

Ages are approximate. Check the prerequisites and sample tasks against your learners’ readiness. A pack covers its named skills; it is not a complete curriculum.

What files are included?

The featured N01–N06 packs include matching A4/US Letter colour and monochrome PDFs, editable teaching text and a file guide. LA01 and LA04 also include editable PowerPoint teaching slides. Editable text is not an editable Word page layout. Check each listing for its exact contents.

Do four print editions mean four different packs?

No. A4/US Letter and colour/monochrome editions repeat the same teaching content in different print formats. Page counts refer to one edition.

Where do I buy and download?

Follow a TPT or Tes link to view the current preview, price, licence and checkout. Payment and delivery are handled by that marketplace. This site does not take payments.

Can I adapt a paid pack?

The featured paid packs use a single-teacher classroom licence allowing adaptation and secure sharing with that teacher’s pupils. Public redistribution or resale is not permitted. Free resources may have different licences; read their listings.

How do I get help?

Email support@openlatitude.net with the pack title, version and page or task number. Please leave out pupil details. For payment or account problems, use the help service of the marketplace where you purchased.
